iOS App: Inventory / Item Tracking

A SwiftUI inventory manager built to track items across multiple locations, log item in/out history, and surface helpful alerts for low stock and upcoming expirations.

SwiftUI SwiftData Local Notifications CSV Export

At a glance

  • Locations → Items with quantities
  • Item in/out transactions + history
  • Quick-add suggestions + tips
  • Configurable low-stock & expiration alerts
  • Profile + export selected locations to CSV

What it is

This app is a personal inventory system that organizes your stuff by Location (Kitchen, Garage, Pantry, etc.) and tracks Items inside each location. The goal is to make it fast to see what you have, where it is, and how quantities change over time.

Core features

Locations

Create, rename, delete, and reorder locations for clean organization.

Items grid

View items in a compact card grid showing name + current quantity.

Item In / Out

Record quantity changes as transactions and keep an item’s running history.

Quick add + tips

Smart suggestions (by location) and optional tips to reduce friction.

Alerts

Low-stock and expiration alerts with global or item-specific thresholds and frequency.

Export

Export selected locations to a CSV for backup or sharing.

How it works (high level)

  • SwiftData models store `InventoryLocation`, `LocationItem`, and `User` on device.
  • Ordering is persisted with an `order` field on locations/items to support rearranging.
  • Transactions record quantity changes (in/out), including optional expiration dates and custom “purchase detail” fields.
  • Smart removals can remove from specific purchase bundles (so expirations/metadata stay attached to the right batch).
  • NotificationManager checks items for low stock / expiration and schedules alerts using global or item-specific thresholds and frequency.

Accounts & settings

  • Sign in / sign up with a profile photo, password requirements, and a security question for recovery.
  • Profile includes app settings (notifications, appearance), support links, and sign out.
  • Export lets you select locations and share a CSV snapshot.
